B. Risk and control summary

Risk

Payments may be released without appropriate authorization, resulting in unauthorized disbursements or inaccurate financial reporting.

Control objective

Payments above the approved threshold are authorized by an appropriate individual before release.

Control description

The payment platform requires dual approval for all payments above $10,000 before funds are released.

Assertions

OccurrenceCompletenessAccuracy

Frequency

Per transaction

Control owner

Finance Operations Manager

C. Audit programme

Test of Design

  1. Obtain the current payment-approval policy and approval matrix.
  2. Verify that approval thresholds, authorized approvers, and segregation-of-duties expectations are documented.
  3. Confirm that the control identifies the control owner, operating frequency, retained evidence, and escalation path.
  4. Conclude whether the control is designed to address the stated risk.

Test of Effectiveness

  1. Obtain the complete population of payments above $10,000 for Q3 2026.
  2. Select a sample using the documented sampling approach.
  3. Inspect each selected payment for evidence of two approvals before payment release.
  4. Verify the approvers were authorized under the current approval matrix.
  5. Record any exceptions and evaluate whether the control operated effectively.
  6. Draft the auditor conclusion.
