Evidence Quality Review & PBC Completeness Centre helps track requested evidence, completeness, and readiness for testing. It does not independently verify source-system accuracy, determine evidence sufficiency, assess control effectiveness, or issue audit conclusions. The Benve auditor is responsible for all evidence evaluation, testing decisions, exception assessment, and audit conclusions.
