Regulatory Update — SEC Proposes 5-Year SOX 404(b) Exemption

A. Update summary

Date24 July 2026
SourceSEC
TagsSOX 404 IPO Readiness
Comment period closes03 August 2026

B. What changed

"SEC proposed amendments to Exchange Act Rules 12b-2 and 13a-1 to create a new 'emerging growth company' (EGC) category with extended SOX 404(b) exemption. Newly public companies would have up to 5 years before requiring external auditor attestation on ICFR, compared to current 2-year EGC exemption."

C. What this means for fintechs

Pre-IPO fintechs: Extended timeline reduces IPO readiness pressure. Companies can focus on 404(a) management assessment first.
Current EGCs: If adopted, existing EGCs may qualify for extended exemption. Monitor final rule.
Investor expectations: Even with exemption, investors may expect 404(b)-ready controls. Benve recommends maintaining audit-ready workpapers.
Timeline impact: IPO in 2027 → SOX 404(b) not required until 2032 (if adopted as proposed).

D. Benve recommendation

"Continue building SOX 404(a) management assessment capabilities. Use extended timeline to strengthen CCM coverage and remediation processes. Do not delay ICFR programme — investor due diligence will still expect robust controls."
