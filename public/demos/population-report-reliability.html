Auditor Conclusion — IPE-001 — High-Value Payment Population

A. Assessment conclusion

Suitable for selected testing procedure Suitable with documented limitations Further procedures or clarification required Not suitable for intended procedure

"The Q3 2026 high-value payment population was obtained directly from the payment platform for the period 01 July 2026 to 30 September 2026. The report parameters were retained. The population count agreed to the payment-platform control total, and the population value agreed to the relevant settlement report. Selected items were agreed to underlying payment records and approval logs. No unsupported transformations or manual changes were identified.

The population is suitable for the approved purpose of selecting transactions for Test of Effectiveness procedures over CTRL-PAY-001. The temporary approver workflow change has been linked to CHG-001 and will be addressed through targeted testing procedures."

Population & Report Reliability Assessment helps document auditor procedures over information produced or provided by the company. It does not independently verify all source-system data, guarantee report completeness or accuracy, determine evidence sufficiency, assess control effectiveness, or issue audit conclusions. The Benve auditor is responsible for designing and performing appropriate procedures, evaluating limitations, and approving the final conclusion.
