Impact summary

The temporary approver workflow has a high potential impact on CTRL-PAY-001 because it may permit a payment above $10,000 to be released without two separate authorized approvals. The change also affects relevant access-management and change-management controls. Targeted testing and control-documentation updates are required before the change can be closed.

Auditor conclusion

No material impact Impact addressed — controls updated and retested Further remediation required Deficiency logged — follow Deficiency & Remediation Tracker

The temporary approver workflow was assessed as a high-impact change. The payment configuration and approval matrix were updated to enforce dual approval. Targeted testing of post-remediation payments confirmed that two authorized approvals were recorded before payment release. No further exceptions were identified in the targeted testing population. CHG-001 is closed, subject to normal ongoing control monitoring.

SOX Change Impact Assessment helps document potential impacts of changes on SOX 404 / ICFR risks and controls. It does not determine whether a change is material, whether controls are effective, or whether a deficiency exists. The Benve auditor is responsible for all impact assessments, testing decisions, deficiency evaluations, and conclusions.
