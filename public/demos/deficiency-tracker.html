DEF-001 — Payment approval exception

In progress
⚠  High priority — remediation is required

A. Related control

Control

CTRL-PAY-001 — Dual Approval for High-Value Payments

Framework

SOX 404 / ICFR

Process

Payments and settlement

Risk

Unauthorized payments may be released without appropriate authorization.

Control owner

Finance Operations Manager

B. Issue documentation

Condition

One of 25 sampled payments above $10,000 did not have evidence of a second approval before release.

Criteria

The approved payment policy requires dual approval for payments above $10,000 before funds are released.

Cause

The approval workflow configuration did not include an escalation rule for a temporary approver.

Effect

Unauthorized payments or inaccurate financial reporting could occur.

Compensating controls

Daily settlement reconciliation identified the payment after release; however, the reconciliation does not prevent an unauthorized payment.

Auditor notes

Management has acknowledged the exception and is updating the approval workflow and approval matrix.

Remediation Plan — Payment Approval Exception

Required action

Update the payment approval workflow and approval matrix so that all payments above $10,000 require approval by two authorized approvers before release.

Why it matters

A sampled payment was released without evidence of the required second approval.
