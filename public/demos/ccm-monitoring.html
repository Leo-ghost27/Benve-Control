Continuous Controls Monitoring (CCM)

Real-time, population-level testing for high-risk, high-frequency controls — moving beyond periodic sampling.

Auditor-only view. Client users and external auditors cannot see CCM configuration or raw evidence logs. They see only summary exceptions and remediation status.
