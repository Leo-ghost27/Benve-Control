Predictive Deficiency Detection enables proactive risk management. AI identifies controls at risk before failures occur. The auditor implements preventive actions to reduce deficiency probability.

Predictive Deficiency Detection uses AI to analyze historical patterns, control owner changes, system changes, and transaction volumes to predict deficiency risk. AI predictions are probabilistic, not deterministic. The auditor is responsible for assessing predictions, implementing preventive actions, and making final risk assessments. AI predictions do not replace actual testing.
