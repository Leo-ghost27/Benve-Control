Material Weakness Evaluation — FY2026 Year-End

A. Material weakness assessment

Management, with support from the Benve engagement team, has evaluated all identified control deficiencies and significant deficiencies, individually and in combination, to determine whether any constitute a material weakness as of 31 December 2026. A material weakness exists when there is a reasonable possibility that a material misstatement of the annual or interim financial statements will not be prevented or detected on a timely basis. For the FY2026 assessment, management considered: - The likelihood and magnitude of potential misstatements - The nature of affected accounts and disclosures - The operation of compensating controls - Aggregation of related deficiencies - Remediation status and evidence as of year-end.

Assessment conclusion

No material weaknesses identified as of year-end One or more material weaknesses identified as of year-end Evaluation in progress — not final

"Management concluded that no individual deficiency or combination of deficiencies represents a material weakness as of 31 December 2026. The identified significant deficiency (DEF-001) was remediated before year-end, and post-remediation testing supported the conclusion that the control operated effectively for the remaining period. Other identified deficiencies were assessed as not rising to the level of a material weakness based on likelihood, magnitude, and compensating controls."

Draft Management's Annual Report on Internal Control over Financial Reporting

A. Draft report text

Management's Annual Report on Internal Control over Financial Reporting

Management of Meridian Pay, Inc. is responsible for establishing and maintaining adequate internal control over financial reporting (ICFR). Management has assessed the effectiveness of ICFR as of 31 December 2026, using the COSO 2013 Internal Control – Integrated Framework. Based on this assessment, management concludes that Meridian Pay, Inc. maintained effective internal control over financial reporting as of 31 December 2026. Management's assessment included an evaluation of the design and operating effectiveness of controls over financial reporting for the processes and accounts in scope for the Company's SOX 404 programme. Management reviewed the results of internal and external testing, deficiency assessments, and remediation activities. No material weaknesses were identified as of 31 December 2026. This report does not include the independent registered public accounting firm's attestation report on ICFR, which will be included in the Company's Annual Report on Form 10-K where required.
