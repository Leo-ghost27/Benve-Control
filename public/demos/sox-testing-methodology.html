SOX Testing Methodology & Sample Planning Centre helps document auditor-led testing methodology, sample planning, sample selection, testing results, and responses to exceptions. It does not determine sample sizes, select samples without auditor review, determine control effectiveness, classify deficiencies, or issue audit conclusions. The Benve auditor and Engagement Lead are responsible for all methodology, testing, evaluation, and conclusion decisions.
