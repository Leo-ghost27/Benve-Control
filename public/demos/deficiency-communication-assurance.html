B. Draft communication

Subject: Q3 2026 ICFR Control Deficiency Update — High-Value Payment Dual Authorization During Q3 2026 testing, an exception was identified in the operation of the high-value payment dual-authorization control (CTRL-PAY-001). One payment of $15,000 was released with one recorded approval when the control required two separate authorised approvals. The exception occurred following implementation of a temporary approver workflow. The control environment, related change record, payment logs, and additional targeted testing were reviewed. Management updated the payment configuration to enforce dual approval for temporary approvers and revised the relevant policy. Post-remediation testing of 25 transactions identified no further exceptions. The Benve Engagement Lead has approved a draft assessment of this matter as a significant deficiency for the demonstration engagement. This communication is provided for authorised management and governance review. Management remains responsible for evaluating internal control over financial reporting and for determining any required disclosures or communications.

Remediation evidence and retesting support the assessment of corrective actions. They do not automatically remove the need for period-end evaluation, management assessment, or authorised audit conclusions.

Controlled Deficiency Communication & Remediation Assurance Pack helps prepare and track approved communications and remediation support related to SOX 404 / ICFR deficiencies. It does not determine required disclosures, provide legal advice, issue external-audit opinions, determine whether a material weakness exists, or replace management's ICFR assessment. The Benve Engagement Lead and authorised client and governance stakeholders are responsible for final communications, disclosures, remediation decisions, and conclusions.
