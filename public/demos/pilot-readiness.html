Control

Severity

Significant deficiency (demo classification)

Status

Description

Payment #PAY-2026-0847 ($12,500) was released with only one approval timestamp. Dual-authorization control was not operating as designed for this transaction.

Root cause (demo)

Temporary approver workflow enabled during approver absence. Secondary approval requirement was bypassed.

Remediation assigned to

Sarah Jones (Payments Manager)

Remediation due date

30 Nov 2026

Remediation plan (demo)

  1. Update payment system configuration to enforce dual approval even for temporary approvers.
  2. Update payments policy to clarify temporary-approver process.
  3. Retest a sample of post-remediation transactions.
