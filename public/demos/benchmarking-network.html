SOX 404 Benchmarking Network — Meridian Pay

A. What is benchmarking?

Benve's Benchmarking Network aggregates anonymized data from participating Benve Control clients to provide industry benchmarks for SOX 404 programmes. Your data is anonymized, aggregated, and never shared with other clients or third parties.

B. Data shared (anonymized)

  • ✓Exception rate by control type (e.g., payments, ITGC, revenue)
  • ✓Testing cycle time (days from test start to completion)
  • ✓Remediation time (days from deficiency logged to closed)
  • ✓Control count by process area
  • ✓CCM coverage (% of controls under continuous monitoring)
  • ✓SOX readiness score trends

C. Data NOT shared

  • Company name or identifying information
  • Specific control descriptions or test steps
  • Individual transaction details
  • Deficiency descriptions or root causes
  • Client or auditor names
  • Financial data or revenues
