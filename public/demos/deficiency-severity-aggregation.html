Selected sample classification: "Significant deficiency — auditor-reviewed draft"

"DEF-001 relates to a high-risk preventive payment-authorisation control. There is a reasonably possible risk that the temporary approver workflow could allow unauthorised high-value payments to be released without timely prevention. While the identified payment amount was below materiality and related detective controls were operating, the issue is important enough to merit attention by those responsible for oversight of financial reporting. The assessment is based on the facts and procedures documented for the demo engagement and requires authorised review before communication."

Deficiency Severity & Aggregation Assessment Centre helps document auditor-led evaluation of identified control exceptions and deficiencies. It does not automatically classify a deficiency, determine whether a material weakness exists, determine materiality, provide legal advice, or issue audit conclusions or opinions. The Benve Engagement Lead and authorised professionals are responsible for all final severity, communication, and reporting decisions.
