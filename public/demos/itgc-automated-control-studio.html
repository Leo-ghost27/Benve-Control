ITGC & Automated Control Monitoring & Reporting

Last 12 months (demo data): - ITGC exceptions: 11 (Access 5, Change 4, Operations 2) - Automated control exceptions: 7 (Payments 3, Revenue 2, Treasury 2) - Open ITGC deficiencies: 2 (Access, Change) - Open automated control deficiencies: 1 (Payments dual approval) - All high-severity exceptions have remediation plans and target dates.

B. Reporting to management and Audit Committee

ITGC and automated control update (illustrative): - 8 in-scope systems; 24 ITGC controls; 14 automated application controls. - No material weaknesses identified in ITGC or automated controls as of year-end. - Two control deficiencies in ITGC remain open with remediation in progress. - Automated controls in payments and revenue continue to operate effectively, with one prior significant deficiency remediated and retested.

C. Linkage to group ICFR and external audit

ITGC and automated control results feed into: - Group ICFR roll-up (Task 47) - Deficiency assessment and aggregation (Task 40) - External auditor coordination hub (Task 44) - Continuous controls monitoring hub (Task 45)

ITGC and automated control monitoring supports ongoing oversight. It does not replace detailed IT audit procedures or management's ICFR assessment.

ITGC Depth & Automated Control Assurance Studio helps document and test IT general controls and automated application controls using illustrative workpapers and sample data. It does not connect to live systems, automatically validate configurations, determine ITGC sufficiency, or replace detailed IT audit procedures. Management and the external auditor are responsible for all final testing, evaluation, and conclusions.
